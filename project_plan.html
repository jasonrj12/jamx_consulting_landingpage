Client Project Plan & Objectives

Project Objectives

The primary aim of this consultancy project is to address low employee engagement and retention due to communication gaps within GreenTech Innovation Pvt Ltd. The objectives are:

1

Analyse Workforce Engagement and Retention

Identify root causes of low employee engagement and high turnover, particularly among technical and site-based staff.

2

Evaluate Communication Effectiveness

Assess internal communication channels, processes, and barriers across departments and regional offices.

3

Assess Workforce Capability

Review workforce skills, identify gaps, and evaluate alignment with operational and project needs.

4

Develop Retention and Engagement Strategies

Recommend practical, evidence-based strategies to improve employee engagement, retention, and collaboration.

5

Propose Training and Development Initiatives

Design targeted learning programs to close skills gaps and support effective communication.

6

Enhance Communication Frameworks

Provide structured frameworks to improve transparency, feedback, and information sharing within the organisation.

7

Ensure Ethical and Feasible Solutions

Recommendations to be ethical, realistic, cost-effective, and aligned with GreenTech's strategic goals.

Project Plan

1

Phase 1: Project Initiation

Week 1–2
  • Kick-off meetings with GreenTech leadership and HR
  • Define scope, objectives, and project deliverables
  • Plan stakeholder interviews, surveys, and data collection
2

Phase 2: Data Collection & Workforce Assessment

Week 3–4
  • Conduct surveys, interviews, and focus groups with employees
  • Collect HR and operational data on engagement, turnover, and skills
  • Document current communication channels and processes
3

Phase 3: Analysis

Week 5–6
  • Analyse employee engagement trends and turnover patterns
  • Identify communication gaps and process inefficiencies
  • Evaluate workforce capability and skills gaps
4

Phase 4: Strategy Development

Week 7–8
  • Develop recommendations for improving engagement, retention, and communication
  • Propose targeted training and development plans
  • Design structured communication frameworks
5

Phase 5: Reporting & Presentation

Week 9
  • Prepare a comprehensive report of findings and recommendations
  • Present findings to GreenTech leadership and HR
  • Provide advisory guidance on implementation priorities and feasibility
