Risk Management

Comprehensive risk assessment and mitigation strategies to ensure project success and protect stakeholder interests.

Risk ID Risk Description Likelihood Impact Mitigation Strategy Risk Owner
R1 Employees reluctant to participate in surveys or interviews Medium High Ensure informed consent, maintain confidentiality, communicate benefits Project Lead / HR
R2 Data collected is incomplete or inaccurate Medium High Cross-verify data, use multiple sources, triangulate findings Project Team
R3 Resistance from management to implement recommendations Medium High Engage stakeholders early, present evidence-based insights Project Lead / Consultant Lead
R4 Confidentiality breaches Low High GDPR-compliant data handling, anonymise responses, secure storage Project Team
R5 Misalignment of recommendations with organisational goals Medium High Validate recommendations with senior management, align with strategy Consultant Lead
R6 Limited access to workforce or operational data Medium Medium Schedule interviews in advance, use surveys, ensure stakeholder support Project Team
R7 Employee disengagement worsens during project due to perceived scrutiny Medium Medium Communicate project purpose clearly, ensure anonymity, involve employees in solutions HR / Project Lead
