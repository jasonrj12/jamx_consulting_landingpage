Communication Plan

The communication plan for the GreenTech consultancy project is designed to ensure timely, clear, and transparent information sharing among all stakeholders.

Project Sponsors & Senior Management

Frequency: Weekly

Email updates and milestone reports

Presentations highlighting key findings and recommendations

HR Department

Frequency: Bi-weekly

Workshops and email updates

Insights into employee engagement, retention trends, and communication gaps

Technical & Site-Based Employees

Townhall Meetings, Emails & Workshops

Project purpose, surveys, and interviews

Ensuring participation and confidentiality

Project Team & Consultants

Frequency: Daily & Weekly

Project management tools and regular meetings

Monitor progress and task completion

Middle Management & Team Leaders

Frequency: Bi-weekly

Reports and workshops

Team-specific findings and suggested actions

External Stakeholders

Project Conclusion

Summary reports or presentations (where applicable)

Communication Principles

Transparency

Open and honest communication

Two-Way Feedback

Encouraging dialogue and input

Confidentiality

Protecting sensitive information

Clarity

Clear and understandable messaging

Consistency

Regular and reliable updates

Engagement

Involving all stakeholders
