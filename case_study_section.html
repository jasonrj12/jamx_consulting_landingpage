How we helped GreenTech Innovation transform their workforce strategy

GreenTech Innovation Pvt Ltd

UK-Based Renewable Energy Organisation

A UK-based renewable energy organisation specialising in solar technology installation and sustainable project expansion. GreenTech has experienced rapid growth driven by increased demand for government-led sustainability targets and clean energy solutions.

The Challenge

Despite rapid expansion, GreenTech faced critical workforce challenges that threatened operational performance and project delivery:

  • Low Employee Engagement: Declining morale among technical staff affecting productivity and quality
  • High Retention Issues: Significant turnover in site-based and technical roles requiring specialized expertise
  • Capability Gaps: Insufficient trained employees to support expanding solar projects
  • Communication Breakdowns: Ineffective communication across departments and operational sites leading to unclear role expectations
  • Project Delivery Impact: Inability to deliver projects within agreed timelines and budgets

Our Approach

JMAX People Consulting Group conducted a comprehensive workforce analysis to identify root causes of employee disengagement and attrition, with particular focus on communication breakdowns and capability development.

Root Cause Analysis

Identified communication gaps and engagement barriers

Workforce Strategy

Designed structured retention and capability framework

Communication Enhancement

Implemented cross-departmental communication protocols

Training Programs

Developed targeted technical and leadership development

Key Deliverables

Workforce Capability Assessment

Comprehensive skills gap analysis

Retention Strategy Roadmap

Long-term employee engagement plan

Performance Frameworks

Clear progression and accountability systems
